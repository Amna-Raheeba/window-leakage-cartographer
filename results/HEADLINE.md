# Headline results

Random 75/25 splits of sliding windows leak 76.1% (+/- 38.3) of test windows on average (pooled over 120 seeded runs, 5 window geometries including a non-overlapping control). The overlap graph detects them at precision 1.000 / recall 1.000; exact-duplicate counting recalls 0.000. The leaky kNN RUL score is optimistic by 1.01 (+/- 0.84) RMSE (leaky 13.64 (+/- 2.42) vs leave-own-unit-out 14.65 (+/- 2.52); purged 15.04 (+/- 2.29), unit-disjoint split reference 14.59), and per-component removal replay recovers 86.4% of the MSE optimism, component by component - the rest is the same-unit adjacency premium. The recovery share is pooled by total squared-error gain across runs (total replay gain divided by total LOUO squared-error gain), not a simple average of the per-geometry shares.
